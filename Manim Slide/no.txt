    Rick Riordan writes a famous modern Greek Mythology book about Percy Jackson: The Lightning Thief. This story makes people debate whether Percy is heroic or reckless. 
From my perspective, I strongly believe Percy Jackson is a heroic demigod. Although people claim Percy is reckless
because he takes risks without thinking, I disagree with their opinions because his reckless actions prove  
his exceptional loyalty and heroic qualities.
    Percy once said: “I had spent thousands of miles worried that I'd be betrayed by a friend, but these friends would never do that. They had done nothing but save me, over and over,
and now they wanted to sacrifice their lives for my mom.” His quote is expressed in chapter 19, page 157. This happens when Percy only
has three magical pearls left, he decides to sacrifice his mother, refuse to abandon one of his friend in the Underworld. This quotes 
demonstrate Percy inabilities to sacrifice the innocents for his personal desire and his main motivation for the quest, saving his mother.
The dilemma demonstrates his selflessness and excessive loyalty between his friends and his desire, which makes him excpetionally heroic.
